# FreshFishing – Modrinth Description

## Short Description (1-2 sentences)
Complete fishing overhaul with dynamic sizes, rarities, economy integration, grappling hooks, and more. Perfect for survival and RPG servers.

---

## Long Description

### Catch bigger, rarer, and more valuable fish

FreshFishing transforms vanilla fishing into a rewarding progression system. Every fish you catch has a **unique size and rarity** — from tiny 6cm catches to massive 600cm beasts. Rarities range from Common to Mystic, each with custom colors and price multipliers.

### Features at a Glance

🐟 **Sizes & Rarities** – Configurable tiers with exponential distribution. Fish scale visually based on tier and rarity.

💰 **Economy Integration** – Open a Fish Market GUI to sell your catches for money. Prices scale with size and include enchantment bonuses. Requires Vault or VaultUnlocked.

🪝 **Grappling Hooks** – Use your fishing rod to swing off walls, grapple entities, or pull yourself toward high ground. Perfect for parkour and exploration.

🦠 **Bait System** – Require specific items (spider eyes, bread, etc.) to fish. Optional consumption. Great for gating fishing early-game.

🏜️ **Sand Loot** – Place markers to auto-populate suspicious sand/gravel blocks with custom loot tables.

🎯 **Random Mobs** – Catch creatures instead of fish (squid, drowned, guardians, creepers). They launch toward you with physics.

💾 **Persistent Data** – Fish size and rarity survive death, buckets, and server restarts via PDC.

🎨 **Full Customization** – Every aspect is configurable via `config.yml`: sizes, rarities, prices, cooldowns, permissions, and more. Full MiniMessage support for colors and gradients.

### Perfect For
- **Survival Servers** – adds depth and progression to vanilla fishing
- **RPG Servers** – economy integration, rarity tiers, loot tables
- **Factions/Skyblock** – fun mob catching and grappling mechanics
- **Mini-games** – fishing challenges, treasure hunts

### Requirements
- Paper 1.21+
- Vault or VaultUnlocked (for economy features)
- Java 17+

No dependencies beyond Vault. Works standalone or as part of a larger ecosystem.
